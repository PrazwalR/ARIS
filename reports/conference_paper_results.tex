\begin{abstract}
This report provides the complete, empirically verified experimental metrics suite for the AI-Agent Risk Integration System (ARIS). ARIS bridges federated learning, privacy-preserving cryptographic attestation, on-premise receiver graph velocity extraction, and real-time AI-agent pre-transaction verification across distributed banking institutions. The benchmarks presented herein cover all Machine Learning evaluation criteria (including full confusion matrices, threshold-invariant metrics, operating characteristic points, and differential privacy accounting) as well as all Backend infrastructure metrics (cryptographic primitive throughputs, tail latency percentiles, prefix-bucketed broker contention, and AES-GCM audit log performance).
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
Federated Learning, Confusion Matrix, Differential Privacy, Byzantine Robustness, Financial Fraud Detection, Shared Risk Bus, AI Banking Agents, Ed25519 Attestation, Latency SLAs.
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Machine Learning Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:ml_metrics}

\subsection{Experimental Setup and Dataset Partitioning}
To evaluate model performance without centralizing customer records, ARIS was benchmarked across three datasets:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Real-World ULB Credit Card Fraud Dataset:} 30,000 transactions partitioned chronologically across 5 simulated banks ($BANK\text{-}A$ through $BANK\text{-}E$). An unseen temporal holdout of $6,000$ transactions ($34$ fraud cases, $0.57\%$ positive rate) was reserved for unbiased out-of-sample evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Synthetic Non-IID Banking Stream:} Partitions with distinct fraud signatures (feature skew) per bank, evaluated on a pooled holdout of $1,200$ transactions ($309$ fraud cases, $25.75\%$ positive rate).
    \item \textbf{Multi-Bank Graph Stream:} Coordinated cross-bank mule accounts receiving rapid incoming velocity bursts, evaluated on $1,200$ holdout transactions ($49$ fraud cases, $4.08\%$ positive rate).
\end{enumerate}
